METHOD / LIMITS / DATA HANDLING

Useful signals.
Honest boundaries.

A transparent first-pass evaluator—not a certification or a substitute for formal privacy analysis.

01 / UTILITY

Moment & proportion match

Numeric fields compare normalized mean and standard-deviation differences. Categorical fields compare category proportions using total variation distance. Overall utility averages the per-feature similarity scores.

02 / PRIVACY

Nearest-neighbor screen

Compares up to 300 sampled synthetic rows with 300 sampled source rows. Numeric distances are scaled by source range; categorical matches contribute exact equality. Reports exact row overlap and average closest-record similarity.

03 / COVERAGE

One selected group

Compares category proportions for the selected categorical column. It highlights missing categories and changes in representation. It does not evaluate fairness or downstream performance.

04 / DATA HANDLING

Browser session only

CSV parsing and calculations happen in the open browser tab. Files are not uploaded. Raw rows are not saved in local storage. Closing or refreshing the tab clears uploaded data.

!
Do not treat these scores as a privacy guarantee.

This prototype does not implement differential privacy, membership-inference attacks, formal disclosure control, or a legal compliance assessment. Tiny or sensitive datasets need expert review.

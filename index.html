METHOD / INTERPRETATION

Clear methods.
Honest limits.

Privacy Prism is an exploratory workbench that helps teams ask better questions before releasing synthetic datasets.

01 / UTILITY

Distribution similarity

Numeric fields use summary-statistic and empirical shape comparisons; categorical fields use total variation distance. This can miss important dependence, tail, or task-specific failures.

02 / PRIVACY

Nearest neighbors & inference

Sampled row similarity and simple known-field inference are risk signals only. They do not represent a complete attacker model, differential privacy, or proof of re-identification.

03 / COVERAGE

Observed group proportions

Single-field and two-field coverage compare observed proportions. They do not define fair treatment, identify all protected groups, or assess downstream harms.

04 / LOCAL-FIRST

Data stays in this tab

Files are parsed in browser memory. Reports include aggregate metrics and filenames, not raw rows. Closing the tab clears the uploaded data.

05 / SAMPLE LIMITS

Small data needs care

Privacy and task-proxy checks cap their comparison sets. Sampling, rare categories, mixed data types, and small groups affect score stability.

06 / NO CERTIFICATION

A gate is not a guarantee

Release policies are user-defined screening thresholds. Passing a gate does not establish legal compliance, secure release, or statistical validity for a particular use.
